Tips for expats

Getting your hair done in Bratislava.

It's more than a haircut. Here's what every expat should know before their first appointment.

✂️
Prices & Tipping

Bratislava hair prices are significantly lower than Western Europe. A men's barbershop cut typically costs €15–€30; a women's cut and blowdry €25–€60; colour from €50–€120+ depending on technique. Tipping is customary — 10–15% is standard and always appreciated. Most salons accept card payment, but smaller barbershops may be cash-only. Ask before you sit down.

🗣️
Communication Tips

The biggest expat frustration is describing exactly what you want. Our listed professionals all speak English, but it helps to bring reference photos — a simple screenshot from Instagram or Pinterest removes any ambiguity. For complex colour work, always book a consultation first (usually free). Slovak salons are generally very skilled — the standard in Bratislava is high, particularly for colour work.

📅
Booking & Etiquette

Most good Bratislava salons are fully booked 1–2 weeks in advance, especially on weekends. Book via phone, WhatsApp, or the salon's online booking system. Cancellations should be made at least 24 hours in advance — no-shows are noticed and may affect future bookings. Walk-ins are generally only possible at barbershops on weekdays. For popular colourists, expect to book 3–4 weeks ahead.

Common questions

Hair & Beauty FAQ

Common questions from expats finding salons in Bratislava.

Are there barbers in Bratislava who specialise in Afro/textured hair?
Yes — though options are more limited than in larger Western cities. Filter by 'Natural hair' or 'All hair types' in our listings. The expat community in Bratislava is growing, and several salons have stylists trained in textured hair. We always indicate in profiles which stylists have specific experience with natural hair, Afro, locs, and protective styles.
How do I describe a specific haircut if I don't know the Slovak term?
Show a photo — seriously, it's the single most reliable communication tool in any salon, regardless of language. All our listed professionals speak English, so you can describe in detail. For haircuts, knowing basic terms helps: 'fade', 'taper', 'layers', 'undercut' are widely understood by English-speaking barbers. For colour, 'balayage', 'highlights', and 'ombre' are internationally understood terms.
What's the average waiting time for an appointment at a quality salon?
For a new client at a popular Bratislava salon, expect 1–2 weeks wait for weekday slots and up to 3 weeks for weekends. Top colourists may have 4+ week waits. Book as soon as you know your schedule. Many salons have cancellation lists — ask to be added if you need an earlier slot. Once you're a regular client, booking is much easier.
Do salons in Bratislava accept international debit/credit cards?
Most salons and barbershops in Bratislava accept Visa and Mastercard. American Express is less widely accepted. Some smaller or independent barbershops are still cash-only — it's worth checking when you book. Tipping by card is not always possible, so having a few euros in cash is useful.
Is there a difference between a 'barbershop' and a 'hair salon' in Slovakia?
Broadly yes — barbershops (barbershopy) specialise in men's cuts, beard grooming, and straight razor shaves. Hair salons (kaderníctvo) handle all genders and offer the full range: cuts, colour, treatments, styling. Unisex salons are increasingly common in Bratislava.
