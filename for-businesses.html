Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. A basic listing is free for good — no card, nothing to pay. You’d only ever pay if you chose to upgrade to Pro.

What does upgrading add?

A full profile (photos, opening hours, website, socials), priority placement in your category, highlighted reviews and a Partner badge.

What's the founding-partner deal?

Join during our launch and get the full Pro profile free for your first 6 months. After that you can stay on the free Basic listing for as long as you like — no obligation.

Who can be listed?

Any business in the Bratislava area that can genuinely help clients in English (or the other languages you choose). Every listing is verified, so the directory stays trustworthy for expats.

Can I edit or remove my listing?

Anytime. Log in with your listing email to edit your profile yourself — we send a secure link, no password needed. Or just email us and we'll help. Your data stays in the EU.
