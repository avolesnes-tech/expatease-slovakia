Good to know

Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. A basic listing is free for good — no credit card, nothing to pay. You only ever pay if you choose to upgrade to Pro.

What does upgrading add?

A full profile (photos, opening hours, website, socials), priority placement in your category, highlighted reviews and a Partner badge.

What's the founding-partner deal?

Join during our launch and get the full Pro profile free for your first 6 months. After that you can stay on the free Basic listing for as long as you like — no obligation.

Who can be listed?

Any business in the Bratislava area that can genuinely serve clients in English (or the other languages you select). Every listing is verified — and listings that don't actually serve clients in English may be removed, so the directory stays trustworthy for expats.

Can I edit or remove my listing?

Anytime — just email us and we'll update or remove it. Your data stays in the EU.
